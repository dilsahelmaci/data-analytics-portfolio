# 📋 User Story Specifications | Sales & Customer Dashboards

This document outlines **the business context and dashboard requirements** that guided the design and development of the *Retail Intelligence: Interactive Sales & Customer Performance Dashboards*.

---

## 🎯 Project Purpose

The company’s management team, including **sales managers, executives, and marketing leads**, requested a Tableau solution to better understand overall business performance.  
The dashboards were designed to help stakeholders monitor **sales growth, profitability, and customer behavior** through an intuitive, interactive interface.

---

## 🧾 Sales Dashboard | User Requirements

### **Dashboard Purpose**
Provide a high-level overview of sales metrics and trends to analyze **year-over-year performance** and identify patterns across product categories and regions.

### **Key Requirements**
- **KPI Overview:** Display total **Sales**, **Profit**, and **Quantity** for both the current and previous year.  
- **Sales Trends:** Show monthly trends for Sales, Profit, and Quantity for each year, highlighting the **highest and lowest months**.  
- **Product Comparison:** Compare Sales and Profit across **product subcategories** for both years.  
- **Weekly Trends:** Include weekly Sales and Profit charts showing **above- and below-average** weeks.  
- **Filters & Interactivity:**  
  - Year filter to explore historical performance.  
  - Product filters for Category and Subcategory.  
  - Location filters for Region, State, and City.  
  - Interactive chart actions to drill down into data.

---

## 👥 Customer Dashboard | User Requirements

### **Dashboard Purpose**
Offer insights into **customer base, loyalty, and profitability** to support marketing and retention strategies.

### **Key Requirements**
- **KPI Overview:** Display total **Customers**, **Orders**, and **Sales per Customer** for both years.  
- **Customer Trends:** Monthly comparison of customer activity, with visual emphasis on highest and lowest months.  
- **Customer Distribution:** Show how customers are distributed based on their **number of orders** (1, 2, 3+).  
- **Top Customers by Profit:** List the **top 10 customers** by total profit, including rank, order count, and last order date.  
- **Filters & Interactivity:**  
  - Dynamic year selection.  
  - Filters for Category, Subcategory, Region, State, and City.  
  - Easy navigation between dashboards.

---

## 🧭 Design & Interactivity Goals
- Allow users to explore **historical data (2020–2023)** easily.  
- Enable **dynamic filtering** for flexible insights.  
- Ensure **smooth navigation** between dashboards.  
- Create **clean, professional visuals** highlighting business performance at a glance.

---

💡 *These specifications guided the creation of both dashboards and ensured that the final Tableau solution aligned with stakeholder expectations and decision-making needs.*
